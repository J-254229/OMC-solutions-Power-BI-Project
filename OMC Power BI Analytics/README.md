# OMC Solutions | Power BI Business Analytics

**Sales Performance | Financial Analysis | Profitability | Business Intelligence**

## Project Overview

An interactive business intelligence solution developed using Microsoft Power BI to provide insights into sales performance, financial health, operational efficiency, and business profitability.

## Business Analysis

- Sales performance and growth analysis (MoM & YoY).
- Branch and product performance comparisons.
- Gross profit and profitability analysis.
- Break-even point (BEP) analysis.
- Contribution margin analysis.
- Margin of safety and financial sustainability.

## Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Business Purpose |
|---|---|
| Net Sales | Measures revenue performance |
| Gross Profit | Evaluates profitability |
| Sales Growth | Tracks business growth |
| Contribution Margin | Measures sales remaining after variable costs |
| Break-even Achievement | Evaluates progress toward break-even |
| Margin of Safety | Measures the buffer before incurring losses |

## Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Data Modelling
- Microsoft Excel

## Project Structure

Developed and maintained in Power BI Project (PBIP) format for better version control and project maintainability.
